4.0 SPECIFIC TERMS FOR USERS
4.1For Students (Applicants)
Must be a Kenyan citizen enrolled in a recognized institution of higher learning accredited by the Commission for University Education (CUE) or TVETA.
Provide accurate and complete personal, academic, and financial details.
Applications must be submitted through the official HELB/ HEF portal or approved channels only.
HELB does not charge any application fees.
4.2 Loan Disbursement:
Tuition fees are remitted directly to the institution, while upkeep is credited to the student’s bank account or mobile money account.
Students are responsible for providing accurate bank or Mobile money details registered under their National ID.
4.3 Repayment:
Loan repayment begins either immediately or within 12 months after the completion of studies, depending on the specific loan product and the terms outlined in the loan agreement.
Interest and penalties apply for late payment, as per HELB guidelines.
4.4 For Past Beneficiaries
Obliged to update HELB on employment status
Must make monthly repayments as scheduled or arrange alternative payment plans in cases of financial hardship.
Default may result in:
i.
Penalties
ii.
Legal enforcement
iii.
Listing with Credit Reference Bureaus (CRB)
4.5 For Institutions of Higher Learning
Must confirm student enrolment before disbursement.
Must notify HELB of any changes in student status (deferment, discontinuation, or graduation, etc).
4.6 For Employers
Legally required under the HELB Act to:
Deduct loan repayments from employees’ salaries.
Remit deductions promptly to HELB.
Provide details of employed loanees when requested.
Non-compliance attracts penalties under the
HELB Act
